Who we are

Rocha's Cleaning ("Rocha's Cleaning," "we," "us") is a residential and commercial cleaning business based in Summerville, South Carolina. This policy covers rochascleaningservice.com and the ways you contact us through it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, you may share:

  • Your name, phone number and, if you choose, your email address
  • Your ZIP code and community
  • Details about your property: type of cleaning, how often, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and any notes you add
  • Your preferred way to be contacted (text, call or email)

We also receive whatever you send us directly by text, phone call or email.

Information collected automatically. This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request) to keep the site secure and working. If you arrive from an ad or a link with campaign tags (such as UTM tags or an ad click ID), those tags, the address of the page you landed on and the referring site are sent with your quote request, so we know which ad or link brought you. After you send a request, your browser stores a small note for that session only, so the same request is not sent twice. It never leaves your device.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your quote request and give you a price range
  • To schedule your in-person walkthrough and your cleanings
  • To communicate with you about the service you booked
  • To keep records of the work we agreed on
  • To protect the website and our business from spam, fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that Rocha's Cleaning may contact you by text message about your quote request, your walkthrough and your scheduled cleanings. These are conversational messages about your service, not marketing.

  • Message frequency varies with your request and schedule.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

Mobile phone numbers and text message consent are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the business:

  • Service providers that work on our behalf, such as our form delivery service (Web3Forms), the lead management system that organizes quote requests, website hosting, and our phone and email providers. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Content loaded by this website. To display fonts, the service area map and the map library, your browser connects to Google Fonts, Cloudflare (cdnjs), Esri and OpenStreetMap. These providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of delivering that content, under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of our clients, our crew or others.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and client records for as long as we need them to provide and document our services, and to meet tax, legal and accounting obligations. Requests that do not turn into a booking are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Security

This website uses an encrypted HTTPS connection, and we limit access to client information to the people who need it to do the work. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Email cleaningrochas@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to keep some records when the law requires it.

Children's privacy

This website is meant for adults arranging cleaning services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as Facebook, Instagram and Google. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or services change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
