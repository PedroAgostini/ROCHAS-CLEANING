Good Questions Before You Book

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every home is different. We send a price range by text first, then confirm your final quote after a quick in-person walkthrough, so there are no surprise charges.

Why do you visit before the first clean?

A short walkthrough lets us see your home, agree on what's included, and give you a price that matches the real work.

Do you offer recurring plans?

Yes. We clean weekly, biweekly, or monthly on a set schedule.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Summerville and clean homes in Nexton, Cane Bay, Goose Creek, Moncks Corner, Hanahan, Daniel Island, Mount Pleasant, Carolina Park, Huger, and nearby communities.

Is there anything you don't do?

We don't take on post-construction cleanup or floor work that requires specialized machines.

When are you available?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm.
